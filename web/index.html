Before you use this calculator

The India CSPH Score is a research and teaching prototype. Its models were trained on synthetic data generated from published summaries, not on real patients, and have not been clinically validated.

Do not use the results to guide patient care. They must not be used to diagnose or exclude portal hypertension, start or change treatment, or decide whether to perform HVPG, endoscopy or any other test.

Do not enter patient names or identifiers. Everything you enter stays in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

    Model and evidence

    What the model is for

    Clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH), a hepatic venous pressure gradient of 10 mmHg or more, drives the first complications of compensated advanced chronic liver disease. Measuring it needs a catheter. Non-invasive Baveno criteria leave about half of patients unclassified.

    The ELM score (Giuffrè et al., J Hepatol 2026) shrank that grey zone with machine learning, but relies on spleen stiffness, which is scarce in India, and included no Indian patients. This prototype explores an India-first alternative.

    How the tiers work

    1. Tier 1 scores every case from liver stiffness, platelets, Child-Pugh, cause, BMI, age and sex.
    2. A Tier 1 rule-out or rule-in is final.
    3. Tier 2 adds spleen stiffness, only for Tier 1 grey-zone cases that have it.
    4. Cases still in the grey zone are flagged for spleen stiffness or HVPG.

    Limitations and intended use

    Trained and tested only on synthetic data. Not clinically validated, not a medical device, and not for diagnosis, treatment decisions or self-assessment. Not applicable to decompensated disease, non-cirrhotic portal hypertension or acute illness. Any real use requires ethics approval, real Indian data with paired HVPG, prospective validation and regulatory review.
